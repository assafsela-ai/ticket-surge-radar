        How Radar detects a surge
        
    A small English-language model groups tickets into six support topics. Low-confidence matches appear as Unclassified. Radar compares the latest window with up to four earlier windows of equal length. A surge needs at least two prior windows, at least four current tickets, at least three above baseline, and at least twice the baseline. A zero baseline can also trigger a new signal.
    
        
    For CSV imports, include created_at and subject or body. The optional ticket_id identifies evidence. Use ISO dates and UTF-8. Limit 10 MB and 10,000 rows.
